Separate source regression: when Vulkan's surface capabilities report
an undefined currentExtent, the presenter treated a measured 0x0 like
a failed client-size query. vk_presenter_sync_test compiled from de4c373
with the regression case failed as the presented counter advanced. With
presenter-zero-client-extent.patch, an unavailable size kept its previous
extent, two zero-sized frames produced no presents or stale readbacks,
and restoring the previous size recovered the expected image colour.
The full source-built vk_presenter_sync_test passed on Wine/XWayland.
This case uses an injected surface capability and a controlled client
extent, with real Vulkan swapchain, synchronization and pixel readback.
